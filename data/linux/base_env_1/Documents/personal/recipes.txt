Quick weeknight meals
=====================

## Garlic butter pasta (15 min)
- Cook spaghetti
- Brown butter with 4 cloves garlic (sliced thin)
- Toss pasta with butter, parmesan, red pepper flakes
- Top with fresh parsley

## Sheet pan chicken thighs (35 min)
- Season thighs with paprika, garlic powder, salt, pepper
- Toss broccoli and sweet potato with olive oil
- 425F for 30 minutes
- Rest 5 min before serving

## Quick black bean tacos (10 min)
- Drain and rinse canned black beans
- Heat with cumin, chili powder, lime juice
- Warm tortillas
- Top with avocado, cilantro, pickled onions, hot sauce

## Miso soup with tofu (10 min)
- Bring dashi (or water + kombu) to simmer
- Add cubed tofu and sliced green onions
- Remove from heat, whisk in 2 tbsp white miso
- Don't boil after adding miso!
