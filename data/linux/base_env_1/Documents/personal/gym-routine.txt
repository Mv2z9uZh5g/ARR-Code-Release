Weekly routine (3x/week)
========================

Day A (Mon/Thu):
- Squat 5x5
- Bench press 5x5
- Barbell row 5x5
- Overhead press 3x8
- Bicep curl 3x10

Day B (Wed):
- Deadlift 3x5
- Pull-ups 4x8
- Dumbbell lunges 3x10/leg
- Plank 3x60s
- Face pulls 3x15

Cardio (Tue/Sat):
- 30 min run or cycling
- Keep heart rate 130-150 bpm

- Bench: 185 lbs
- Deadlift: 275 lbs
- OHP: 125 lbs

Goal by June:
- Squat: 250
- Bench: 200
- Deadlift: 315
